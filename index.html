Find it. Pick it. Book it.

Cinema-X is the online guide and ticket booking site for our Melbourne cinema. See what's showing this week, get film suggestions based on who you're watching with, and book your seats without queuing at the box office.

How it works

  1. 01

    Browse movies

    The Movies page lists all 9 films now showing. Each one shows its genre, age rating, running time, a short description, ticket price and session times, so you can compare them before you book.

  2. 02

    Get a recommendation

    Not sure what to watch? Answer three questions: your preferred genre, the age group watching, and who you're watching with. We match your answers against every film's genre and rating and suggest the three best fits.

  3. 03

    Book tickets

    Choose a film, date and time, number of tickets and ticket type (Adult, Child or Student). The total updates as you go. Pay online by Visa, Mastercard or Amex, or with a 12-digit Cinema-X voucher.

Who it's for

  • Families

    Every film shows its Australian age rating. Choose "Child" on the Recommend page and we only suggest films rated G or PG.

  • Students

    Student tickets are 15% cheaper than adult tickets. Choose "Student" as your ticket type when you book.

  • Couples and friends

    Choose "Date night" or "Friends" on the Recommend page to get films suited to your group, such as comedies, romances and action films.

  • Film fans

    Save films to your watchlist as you browse, then come back to compare and book them later.

Why book with Cinema-X

  • Convenience

    Browse, choose and book in one visit, then skip the box office queue on the day.

  • Variety

    9 films across 6 genres, from PG family animations to MA15+ dramas.

  • Accessibility

    Age ratings on every film, a site that works with a keyboard and screen reader, and a special requests box for wheelchair spaces when you book.

  • Entertainment

    This week's big releases on the big screen, from Pixar animation to IMAX-scale science fiction.
